Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This page explains what happens to your information when you use Dream Interpreter Wheel.

What you type in

If you describe a dream in the text box, that text is sent to an AI service solely to generate your interpretation. We do not store, log, or review what you write — it's used once, in the moment, to produce your reading, and then discarded. Nothing you type is saved on our servers.

No accounts, no personal data collection

There's no sign-up, login, or personal information required to use this site. We don't collect names, emails, or other identifying details through normal use of the wheel.

Cookies and advertising

This site may display ads served through Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can learn more, or opt out of personalized advertising, through Google's Ads Settings.

Analytics

We may use basic, privacy-respecting analytics (such as visit counts and general location by country) to understand how the site is used. This data is aggregated and not tied to your individual identity.

Third parties

We don't sell or share your dream text or usage data with third parties, aside from the AI service used to generate readings (necessary for the tool to function) and any advertising or analytics partners noted above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update date at the top of this page will change accordingly. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out via the Contact page.
