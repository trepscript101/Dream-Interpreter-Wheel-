Frequently Asked Questions

Is this real psychology, or just made up?

Readings are grounded in well-established dream psychology — mainly Jungian and Freudian traditions — not invented on the spot. That said, this is a tool for reflection and entertainment, not professional or clinical advice.

Is my dream kept private?

Yes. What you type is sent only to generate your reading and isn't stored, logged, or reviewed by anyone. See the Privacy Policy for full details.

How are readings actually generated?

Each reading is generated by AI, guided by real dream-symbolism research rather than left to invent meaning freely. That's why two people dreaming about the same symbol can get different, specifically personal readings.

Why did I get a different reading for the same symbol?

That's intentional. The underlying meaning of a symbol stays grounded and consistent, but the reading itself is written fresh each time, shaped around whatever detail you add — not a fixed script repeated back to everyone.

Is it free to use?

Yes, spinning the wheel and getting readings is completely free.

Do you save my past readings or track a streak?

No account or history is kept. Each visit starts fresh.

Can I use the wheel every day?

Yes — there's no limit. Come back any time you've got a new dream to make sense of.

My symbol isn't on the wheel — what do I do?

Skip the wheel entirely and just type your dream directly into the text box — it'll generate a reading based on exactly what you describe, no symbol required.
