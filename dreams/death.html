Dreaming About Death — What It Really Means

This is the single most misread symbol in dream interpretation, so it's worth saying plainly: death in a dream is almost never literal, and it's not a warning. Across essentially every serious school of dream psychology, it represents an ending — a phase, an identity, a relationship, or a way of being that's closing to make room for what comes next.

Jung considered death dreams among the most psychologically positive dreams a person can have. The death of an old version of yourself is what allows a more complete, more integrated version to take shape. It's uncomfortable precisely because something real is ending, not because something bad is coming.

These dreams cluster tightly around genuine life transitions — job changes, breakups, big decisions, moves. If you've had one recently, the more useful question isn't 'what does this predict,' but 'what has actually already ended in my life that I haven't fully acknowledged yet.'
