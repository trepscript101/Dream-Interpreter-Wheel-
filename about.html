About Dream Interpreter Wheel

Dream Interpreter Wheel is a simple, independent tool for exploring what your dreams might mean. Spin the dial for a random symbol, tap a symbol directly if you already know what you dreamed, or just describe your dream in your own words — either way, you'll get a short, modern interpretation in return.

We built this because most dream dictionaries feel outdated — long lists of symbols with dry, one-size-fits-all meanings. This is meant to feel lighter and more personal: quick enough to use daily, specific enough to actually be useful, and a little fun along the way.

What this is — and isn't

Readings here are meant for reflection and entertainment, not as psychological, medical, or professional advice. Dreams are deeply personal, and no automated tool can know your life the way you do — think of each reading as a starting point for your own thinking, not a final answer.

How readings are generated

Interpretations are generated using AI, guided by instructions designed to keep readings specific and grounded in well-known dream-symbolism patterns rather than vague, generic text. Nothing you type is stored or reviewed by us — see our Privacy Policy for details.
